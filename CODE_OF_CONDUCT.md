# Code of Conduct

AbyssDB is a joke. The way we treat each other is not.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Good:

- Being kind, patient and constructive, in issues, reviews and discussions
- Assuming good intent, and giving and accepting feedback gracefully
- Laughing with people, never at them
- Helping newcomers, including those who opened an issue about `(0 rows)`

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks, in public or private
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission
- Trolling, or derailing discussions on purpose
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (the repository, issues, pull
requests, discussions and the website) and when someone represents the project
elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer,
[@noctarius](https://github.com/noctarius), privately. All reports are reviewed
promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the reporter is respected. Unlike
everything else in this project, reports are kept.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues that
violate this code, and may ban contributors temporarily or permanently for
repeated or severe violations.
